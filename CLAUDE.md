## Domain Learnings

### Base Home Game Rules

- Official base Hide + Seek rules use 500 m hiding-zone radii for small and medium games, and 1 km for large games; a prior playthrough summary's 400 m value is not the base-rule value. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, checked 2026-10-21]
- The printed investigation booklet has six categories and 80 base-game question prompts with scale-specific availability. The workbook's extra 4th Admin (Borough) Measuring row is excluded from the generated base pack and reported as an informational source discrepancy; the 4th Admin Matching prompt remains. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04-10-56.pdf`; `packages/content-import/src/mapping-profiles/jet-lag-workbook.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- The current app has real first-pass map, question, card, chat/evidence, movement, and match-engine flows, but its nearby adapter has no LAN transport and its online client has no trusted server-side command gateway. [source: repository inspection, 2026-10-21]
- The single-device referee is implemented as a fallback and has a direct Home entry point; its complete round is covered by a real-runtime integration test. On 2026-10-24 the product owner clarified that the intended primary game is online with one phone per player, so the referee mode is not a substitute for the required multiplayer authority. [source: `apps/mobile/tests/single-device-round-flow.test.ts`; product-owner clarification, 2026-10-24]
- Player-facing screens now use the approved Transit Field Kit: paper/ink/signal colors, route-stop navigation, an illustrated launch screen, named Hider/Seeker spaces, and focused clue/card/chat surfaces. [source: `knowledge/game-ui-redesign-research.md`, validated 2026-10-23]
- Host authority comes from host-admin scope, not the player's assigned Hider/Seeker role; UI gating should preserve referee controls when the host also plays a team member. [source: `apps/mobile/tests/player-flow.test.ts`, 2026-10-23]
- A player's role assignment must be reflected by exactly one team membership. The reducer removes the player from their old team's member list before adding the new assignment, which is required for round rotation. [source: `tests/engine/gameplay-rules.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Match start records a randomized order for the remaining seeker players; the initial hider is still selected in role setup. Round start events persist the next hider, shuffled deck, reset round state, and the previous found coordinates for map continuity. [source: `tests/engine/round-rotation.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Time bonus cards cannot be played in the base rules. Their scale-adjusted values count toward final hiding time only while still held when the hider is found; discarded bonuses do not count. The score keeps elapsed survival and bonus value separate. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, pp. 5–6; `tests/engine/hiding-zone.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- If a hider misses a question's response deadline, scored hiding time pauses at the exact deadline and resumes when the answer arrives; a late answer earns no cards. The deadline is 5 minutes for ordinary questions, and 10/20 minutes for Photo questions by scale. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, p. 5; `tests/engine/endgame-questions.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Curse of the Drained Brain lets the hider choose three exact question prompts, each from a different category, that seekers cannot ask for the rest of that hider's run; other prompts in those categories remain available. The lock resets when the next hider starts. Hiders resolve their own card windows online, while the host retains referee control. [source: cleaned workbook Curses sheet; `tests/engine/gameplay-rules.test.ts`; `tests/engine/round-rotation.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Curse of the Overflowing Chalice grants three hider-side question reward opportunities to draw one extra card without changing the keep count. Each eligible reward deal consumes one opportunity whether or not the hider opts in, and the effect resets on hider rotation. [source: cleaned workbook Curses sheet; `tests/engine/question-reward.test.ts`; `tests/engine/round-rotation.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- The “Draw 1, Expand 1” power-up draws one card and permanently raises the hider team's hand limit for the rest of that round; the effect can be stacked. The authority applies it immediately without disrupting an open answer window, projects the limit only to hiders/referee, and resets it when the next round starts. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, p. 46; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Curse of Spotty Memory makes the authority roll one public blocked question category when the card resolves, rejects questions in that category, and rolls again after each accepted question; the same category may be rolled again. The lock resets when the next hider starts. [source: cleaned workbook Curses sheet; `tests/engine/gameplay-rules.test.ts` and `tests/engine/round-rotation.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Curse of the Hidden Hangman uses the scanned five-letter real-dictionary-word rule (base rulebook p. 56): the hider chooses the word privately, seekers’ letters and masked progress are public, and the hider confirms the group-played Hangman game has ended before the card window closes. The engine locks seeker location updates/questions during the challenge. No incorrect-guess limit is invented; the group’s Hangman loss rule and actual transit boarding remain table-enforced. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, p. 56; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Curse of the Bridge Troll (base rulebook printed p. 50) blocks the next question until every active seeker individually marks that they are under the qualifying bridge; each player can update their status if they leave. The accepted next question clears the condition. The app does not infer a person's body position or decide whether a structure qualifies as a bridge. The scan also requires removing this card from maps with no bridges; this pregame deck filter is still open, and missing OSM data cannot prove that a map has none. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, p. 50; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Each participant's foreground location watch now starts automatically during hide, seek, and endgame after location permission is granted, then stops outside active play. Background location is not implemented yet. [source: `apps/mobile/src/providers/LocationSharingProvider.tsx`; `apps/mobile/tests/location-flow.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- The old development RLS migration grants anon/authenticated blanket access to match tables and the attachment bucket; follow-up migration `202610240004_secure_online_access.sql` removes those grants and scopes attachments to active membership plus public/team/player/host identity. All migrations applied in order to a throwaway PostgreSQL 16 database with minimal Supabase Auth/Storage stubs; RLS reads and uploads were checked as host, hider, seeker, outsider, and removed member. This does not replace applying and testing against hosted Supabase's real Auth/Storage services. [source: migrations `202603160002`, `202610240001`, and `202610240004`; local PostgreSQL scope test, checked 2026-10-25]
- OSM administrative levels are country-specific, and Nominatim reverse geocoding returns the nearest suitable indexed object rather than guaranteeing the object contains the coordinate. Do not automate admin-region answers from centroids or send frequent exact player coordinates to public Nominatim; use a bounded, privacy-aware boundary source and verify containment. [source: https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:admin_level; https://nominatim.org/release-docs/latest/api/Reverse/; checked 2026-10-24]
- Veto ends the current response without an answer or card reward but counts toward repeat cost; Randomize cancels the original and starts a same-category unasked replacement, leaving the original eligible at its former cost. A randomized null answer is allowed, resolves without map geometry, and still counts for the hider reward. Both effects are typed authoritative pack data and use public question lifecycle events. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, p. 46; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, 2026-10-24]
- Discard 1 Draw 2 and Discard 2 Draw 3 import as typed authoritative effects; the engine enforces the printed discard count before dealing replacements. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, p. 46; `tests/engine/gameplay-rules.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Each Duplicate still in the hider's hand at capture copies the largest time bonus still held, adding that value again to final hiding time. In-round, hiders select another card; typed supported power-up effects resolve through the authority, while manual challenges display the copied card privately. [source: local scan `~/Downloads/2026-10-04 10-19.pdf`, pp. 25–26; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts` and `tests/engine/hiding-zone.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-24]
- Move and Duplicate are assisted card effects: the engine enforces their supported state changes, and the content pack should not label them manual. Duplicating a manual challenge still opens that copied challenge's normal resolution window. [source: `packages/engine/src/commands/execute-command.ts`; importer, engine, and mobile card-guidance tests, checked 2026-10-25]
- The Move card's scanned procedure is implemented through relocation: seekers are frozen, the original station is revealed, the hider's remaining hand is discarded, only the hider can move, and the authority requires a different station plus arrival before closing; its interval is excluded from final survival scoring. The map uses a fresh device GPS fix within 100 m for arrival and shows the enforced timer. Small/Medium/Large durations (10/20/60 minutes) come from the community card transcription because the scan/workbook omit them. [base rulebook scan p. 26; https://jetlag.naenae.cat/, checked 2026-10-24]

- Landmass Matching requires connected land areas: mapped island/islet polygons can answer automatically, with a hider landmass fully surrounded by the seeker landmass counting as a match; continents, incomplete polygons, and GPS fixes overlapping shorelines remain manual. The same complete polygons drive the approximate map deduction. [source: scanned base rulebook printed p. 20; OSM `place=island|islet` provider and engine regression tests, checked 2026-10-25]

- Urban Explorer is an assisted, hider-run restriction. Each seeker reports whether they are on transit or on/inside a station platform/building; any missing or blocked status prevents the next question. The status is public to the teams and resets on hider rotation; physical location truth remains self-attested. [source: base rulebook scan, printed p. 56; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Water Weight is an assisted hider-run condition. Seekers report acquisition of new liquid totaling 2 L per seeker before questions unlock; a later reported loss adds the printed 30/30/60-minute bonus to round scoring once for that challenge. The 3 m abandonment test and physical volume are group-attested because individual containers are not tracked. State resets on hider rotation. [source: base rulebook scan, printed p. 52; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, `tests/engine/hiding-zone.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Endless Tumble is an assisted challenge: the hider reports its casting die (5/6 cancels; 1–4 activates), then a seeker reports a 30 m parallel-to-ground unaided roll and die result; a lost die or one that does not land cleanly on a side cannot count. Questions stay gated until a clean 5/6 succeeds. Each reported hit adds the printed 10/20/30-minute hider bonus immediately. The physical distance, die, landing, and hit remain player attestations. The engine also prevents two typed question- or transit-blocking curses from overlapping. [sources: base rulebook scans, printed pp. 49, 56; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, `tests/importer/importer.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Lemon Phylactery is an assisted hider-run challenge: each seeker attests that a real lemon is attached to their outermost clothing or skin, and all seekers must report contact before the next question. Every reported contact loss immediately adds the selected-scale 30/45/60-minute hider bonus, and that seeker must reattach before another question. Physical lemon contact remains player-attested. [source: base rulebook scan, printed p. 55; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, `tests/importer/importer.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Gambler’s Feet is an assisted 20/40/60-minute movement restriction: an even hider casting roll cancels it and an odd roll activates it. Each seeker may track an independent d6 allowance; recorded steps spend that allowance, accidental oversteps create a debt that subsequent rolls must cover before more steps, and safety steps are explicitly attested. This card has no printed endgame prohibition. Physical steps and safety judgments remain player-attested. [sources: base rulebook scans, printed pp. 55–56 and the workbook card row; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, `tests/importer/importer.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Egg Partner is an assisted challenge: seekers confirm acquiring a real egg before the next question; the egg counts as a seeker for other curses affecting every seeker. A seeker or referee may report the egg or a seeker abandoned, or the egg cracked; the first reported loss awards the printed scale bonus once. The card cannot be played in endgame. [sources: cleaned workbook Curses sheet and base rulebook curse clarification; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
- Jammed Door is an assisted 30/60/180-minute curse with a shared per-doorway 5/10/15-minute retry cooldown and a 2d6 threshold of 7. Roll only when the doorway is visible; the app records player/referee-attested door identity and dice while enforcing cooldown and expiration. [sources: cleaned workbook Curses sheet; base rulebook clarification, printed p. 50; `tests/engine/cards.test.ts`, checked 2026-10-25]
